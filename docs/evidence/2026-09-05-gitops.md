## Memory findings and bounded corrections

Argo repo-server was OOM-killed during concurrent cold Helm rendering under the generic 256Mi limit. Its old cgroup reported `oom=1`, `oom_kill=1`, peak `268443648` bytes against a `268435456`-byte cap. Automatic retry temporarily converged. The local bootstrap correction uses one rendering slot, a 768Mi container cap and a 500MiB Go target. After rolling repo-server and hard-refreshing the Prometheus Application, its new cgroup reported zero OOM/max events and a `207593472`-byte peak. This control plane is bootstrap-managed, not managed by the root Application; the local correction still needs its source PR reviewed for reproducibility.

Tempo 2.9.0 later exceeded its 384Mi cap while completing its first delayed trace block, then failed again during WAL replay/completion. The original Pod was still present with six OOM-related restarts at **19:02**; other add-ons and dev remained ready. The earlier successful trace correlation was genuine but does not establish current Tempo health. No live Tempo configuration, WAL or PVC was manually replaced/deleted after GitOps adoption.

The proposed Tempo values constrain flush/query/read concurrency, use 8Mi blocks and 4Mi Parquet row groups, and raise its cap to 768Mi with a 500MiB Go target. `bash scripts/test-tempo-memory.sh colima-k3s-lab` passed against the chart's exact image and rendered configuration, with only storage paths changed for isolation:

- Two batches of 5,000 synthetic traces completed/flushed to local storage.
- A persisted trace was found after a container restart.
- Recorded cgroup peaks were `127787008` and `223862784` bytes after the two flushes, and `194506752` bytes after restart/query; recorded OOM/max counters were zero.
